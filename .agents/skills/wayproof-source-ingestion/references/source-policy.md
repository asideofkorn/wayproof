# Source and salvage policy

Consult `DATA_LICENSE.md` before adding source artifacts or copied material.

## Source selection

- Prefer official agencies, facility operators, statutes, maps, and booking
  systems for operational facts.
- Use secondary and social sources for the facts they can actually support,
  especially dated field conditions, without treating them as universally
  authoritative.
- Preserve source referrals: one official page may deliberately defer a topic
  to another publisher or operational system.
- Capture only the excerpt or structured fact needed for evidence. Do not copy a
  whole copyrighted work into the repository.

## Legacy salvage

Classify fields, not whole files:

- `KEEP`: sufficiently understood and sourced candidate data; still promote it
  only through the normal lifecycle.
- `REVERIFY`: potentially useful value or identity requiring a current source
  before promotion.
- `RECONSTRUCT`: a useful concept whose original representation is composite,
  inferred, or incompatible; rebuild it through the current evidence model.
- `DISCARD`: unsupported, duplicated, misleading, presentation-only, or unsafe
  material that should not enter canonical knowledge.

Do not bulk-migrate the legacy corpus. Never carry forward inferred access,
timeless water availability, assumed permit mechanics, booking-system leakage,
or prose notes as canonical facts.
